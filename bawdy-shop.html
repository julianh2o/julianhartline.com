Founded January 2025

The Bawdy Shop

A community and event space that hosts queer-centered events and workshops aimed at fostering community and safety.

Our mission

We endeavor to foster community with robust, meaningful, and examined interpersonal relationships and to create a space that is welcome to all, focused on queer exploration and celebration which enables individuals to grow, collaborate, connect, and create.

Bow was a Java applet archery game. Players clicked and dragged from within the green zones to launch arrows at incoming enemies, with bonus points awarded for headshots.

Cash earned in battle bought upgrades and ammo from the store, and multi-shots and volleys traded arrows for extra firepower.

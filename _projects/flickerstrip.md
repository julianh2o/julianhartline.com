Flickerstrip was a WiFi-controlled LED strip that let you create and upload patterns from your phone or desktop computer.

Born out of a passion for smart home gadgets and inspired by the next generation of NeoPixel art, Flickerstrip was the first pattern-capable LED strip available on the consumer market. The goal was to make LED art accessible to everyone, not just programmers and tinkerers.

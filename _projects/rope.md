Rope was an interactive physics simulation written as a Java applet. You could grab any point on the rope and drag it around, toggle gravity, pin one end in place, and switch between rope, slinky, and line rendering modes with optional dynamic coloring.

It was a fun way to explore wave behavior: standing waves, reflection off a fixed end, constructive interference, and compression waves through a stretched slinky.
